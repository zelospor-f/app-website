Child Safety Standards and Prevention of Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE)

ZELO Sport strictly prohibits any content or behavior involving the sexual abuse or exploitation of children (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming of children, and encouraging any of this.

1. Age group

  • The app is intended for adults only (18 and over). Anyone under 18 is not allowed to use it.
  • We ask for a date of birth when an account is created, and we may suspend any account whose owner turns out to be under the allowed age.

2. Prohibited content and behavior

  • Any sexual content involving children, in any form (images, video, text, voice messages, avatars).
  • Grooming children or contacting them for the purpose of exploitation.
  • Sharing links to, requesting, or offering material of this kind.

The prohibition covers posts, comments, rooms, private messages, profile pictures and usernames.

3. How to report

  • Inside the app: use the "Report" option on any post, comment, room or user. You can also block any user.
  • By email: support@zelosport.net

4. What we do when a report arrives

  • We review child-safety reports as a priority.
  • We remove the violating content and permanently suspend the responsible account.
  • We report these cases to the relevant authorities and cooperate with them as the law requires.

5. Legal compliance

We comply with all applicable child-safety laws and submit reports to the relevant regional and national authorities when necessary.

6. Contact

The contact responsible for these standards and for responding to them:
support@zelosport.net
