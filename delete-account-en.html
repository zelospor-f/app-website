Delete your account and data

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Option 1: inside the app

  1. Open the ZELO Sport app and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Delete account.
  3. Confirm the deletion.

Option 2: by email

If you cannot sign in, email us at support@zelosport.net from the email registered on the account, write "Account deletion request", and include your username. We will process the request and confirm once it is done.

What is deleted

  • Account and profile data (email, name, date of birth, picture).
  • Posts, comments, messages, predictions, and video files linked to your account.

What we may keep

  • Purchase and withdrawal records for the period required by law or to prevent fraud and handle disputes.
  • Security data needed to prevent abuse, which is never shown publicly.
Before you delete:
  • Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Your diamond and coin balances will be lost.
  • Complete any pending USDT withdrawal before deleting your account.
  • Deleting your account does not cancel your Google Play subscription. Cancel it in the Google Play Store to stop renewals.
