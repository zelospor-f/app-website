Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This policy explains what data the ZELO Sport app collects and how we use it. By using the app you agree to it.

1. Data we collect

  • Account data: email address, username, display name, date of birth, language, and a profile picture if you add one.
  • Activity data: posts, comments, messages in rooms and private messages, predictions, support sent and received, Kora earned (including from ads and invites), post boosts and digital card activity, and reports.
  • Files you upload: photos, videos and voice messages you post or send inside the app.
  • Notification data: the device token needed to send you notifications (such as match, follow, comment and message notifications), and your notification preferences, such as the leagues you choose.
  • Invite data: when you install through an invite link from Google Play, we read the invite code attached to the link so we can record who invited you and grant them the invite reward.
  • Room media: voice messages, photos and videos you send in match rooms are stored privately, are visible only to room members, and are automatically deleted when the room ends.
  • Purchase data: purchases and subscriptions are made through Google Play. We do not see or store your card details. We receive proof of the transaction from Google (purchase token, product, and status) to verify it and activate what you bought.
  • Withdrawal data: when you request a withdrawal of your Kora balance we collect your receiving wallet address (USDT on BNB Smart Chain), the amount, and the request status. We may ask for additional verification data to prevent fraud.
  • Approximate country: we infer your country (a two-letter code only, such as TR) from your connection address (IP) when you open the app. We keep only the code, not your IP address, city or precise location. We do not ask for location permission.
  • Device and usage data: advertising ID and general technical information collected through the advertising services listed below.

2. What other users can see

Other users can see your display name, username, picture, favorite club, follower count, posts and comments. Your date of birth is shown only if you choose to show it in your profile settings. The statistics of your account on the "My stats" page are visible to you only.

3. How we use data

  • Operating and protecting your account and sign-in.
  • Showing content, predictions, player rankings and your account statistics.
  • Sending the notifications you asked for.
  • Verifying purchases and activating subscriptions and paid items.
  • Processing and verifying withdrawal requests.
  • Preventing fraud and abuse and enforcing our terms.
  • General statistics and compliance with local laws (using the approximate country).
  • Showing ads and answering support requests.

4. Third parties that process data

  • Supabase: database hosting, authentication, and storage of voice messages.
  • Cloudflare: storage and delivery of the photos and videos you upload (Cloudflare R2).
  • IP-to-country lookup service: receives your connection address momentarily only to determine the country; we do not keep it.
  • Google: sign-in with Google when you choose it, Google Play Billing for payments, Google Play Install Referrer to read invite codes, and Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver notifications.
  • Google AdMob: shows ads in the app and may use the advertising ID and device data. You can manage ad personalization in your device settings.
  • BNB Smart Chain: used to carry out USDT withdrawals. It is a public network and its transactions are visible to everyone.

We do not sell your personal data.

5. Retention

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account we delete your personal data as described on the Delete Account page, except what we must keep by law or to prevent fraud, such as purchase and withdrawal records.

6. Your rights

You may ask to access, correct, or delete your data, and you can withdraw your consent to notifications at any time in the app or device settings. Depending on where you live (including Türkiye's KVKK and the EU/UK GDPR) you may have additional rights. You can delete your account inside the app or by emailing support@zelosport.net.

7. Security

We use technical safeguards such as server-side access controls. No protection is absolute. Keep your password safe and do not share it.

8. Age

The app is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you know of a child's account, contact us so we can delete it. See also our Child Safety Standards.

9. Changes

We may update this policy and will announce material changes in the app or on this page.
